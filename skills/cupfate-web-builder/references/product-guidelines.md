# CUPFATE product guidelines

## Product promise

CUPFATE（纸杯命运）is a small bilingual tarot experience for idle moments, gentle reflection, and friend-to-friend sharing. It should feel like opening a paper cupcake: light, warm, brief, and pleasant. It is entertainment, not an authority on the user's life.

## Required experience

- Reading contexts: 今日运势 / Daily reflection and 心中一问 / Hold a question in mind.
- Draw sizes: one card and three cards.
- Default deck: the 22 Major Arcana.
- Languages: complete Simplified Chinese and English UI and card interpretations.
- Sharing: native system sharing when supported, with copy-link fallback.
- Accessibility: semantic controls, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion respect, and comfortable touch targets.

## Interpretation style

Write interpretations as prompts for reflection:

- Prefer “可能提醒你……”, “可以留意……”, and “不妨思考……”.
- Prefer “may invite you to notice…”, “consider…”, and “one possible perspective is…”.
- Connect advice to ordinary, low-risk actions such as pausing, observing, communicating, or organizing priorities.
- Keep upright and reversed meanings nuanced; reversed cards are not automatically bad.

Avoid:

- Guaranteed predictions, supernatural certainty, or claims of special authority.
- Medical, legal, financial, fertility, safety, or mental-health determinations.
- Fear-based wording, dependency cues, repeated-pay mechanics, or pressure to draw again.
- Telling users to make consequential decisions solely because of a card.

Use a visible equivalent of this disclaimer in both languages:

> 塔罗仅供娱乐与自我反思，不预测确定结果，也不替代专业建议。

> Tarot is for entertainment and self-reflection only. It does not predict certain outcomes or replace professional advice.

## Visual system

- Base: warm cream or misty pale blue.
- Accents: muted butter yellow, dusty blue, sage, and restrained rose.
- Surfaces: quiet cards with soft borders and limited shadow.
- Motifs: cupcake wrapper pleats, paper cups, crumbs, stars, or a small candle used sparingly.
- Typography: clear contemporary type with optional restrained serif display text.
- Motion: short, gentle, and non-essential; honor reduced-motion settings.

Avoid saturated gradients, neon colors, heavy occult imagery, casino-like effects, excessive animation, and dense dashboards.

## Scope defaults

Keep the first version client-side, privacy-preserving, and usable without an account. Do not store the user's private question. Add backend services only when explicitly required.
